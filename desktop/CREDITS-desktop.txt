Wolf80 {version} - who made what is inside

The emulator (its processor cores, the emulated boards and the user interface) is by
Matěj "Wolf" Holyszewski, made with the help of the AI Claude (Anthropic), and is
licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later (LICENSE.txt).

The monitors and many of the programs listed here are the work of other authors. They are
included with credit and are not covered by the emulator's licence. The same credits are
in the application, under "About the emulator" and in the notes of each program. Thanks to
Nostalcomp for preserving and sharing much of this software.


MONITORS (ROM images)

PMI-80
  Factory monitor
      TESLA Piešťany, 1982. The commonly shared image, as published by Nostalcomp
      (https://www.nostalcomp.cz/), who compared it with the ROM of an original PMI-80.
  PMI-80 C
      Nostalcomp's adaptation of the factory monitor (2019), with a second EPROM holding
      the games COSMOS and TENIS3; its scrolling text was changed for this emulator.

Savia-84
  Factory monitor
      Ing. Václav Kraus, for the Savia-84 designed by Jaroslav Tomáš Hyan; printed in the
      book Radioamatérské konstrukce 4 (SNTL, Prague 1990). The image was shared by
      Nostalcomp.

Stone 1 (a computer by Radek Veselý, http://www.malecky.cz/stone1/)
  STONE-PMI
      Nostalcomp's adaptation of the PMI-80 factory monitor for the Stone 1 (2019), with
      the games COSMOS and TENIS3.


PROGRAMS

"Ported by the Wolf80 project": the game was written anew for these computers after the
rules of the original. The game itself is the work of the authors named; the new program
is the emulator author's (GPL-3.0-or-later).
"Moved to RAM" or "moved to the Savia-84": the original program was adapted to run there.
It stays the work of its authors.
